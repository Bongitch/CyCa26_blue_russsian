Sofia Pavlenko

Sofia Pavlenko, 66 years old, a retired woman from Omsk. She has lived her entire life in Omsk — worked as a saleswoman in a grocery store and later as a warehouse worker in a tire factory, raising two children, the younger of whom, her son Konstantin (everyone called him Kostya), became a contract soldier. When he left for the special military operation in February 2022. Kostya was killed in August 2023 near Bakhmut — officially fell heroically in battle; since then Sofia has worn a black headscarf, visits his grave at the Staro-Severnoye Cemetery, sits on the bench by his headstone, strokes the tiles, and tells him about the weather in Omsk or how the neighbor planted potatoes again.
