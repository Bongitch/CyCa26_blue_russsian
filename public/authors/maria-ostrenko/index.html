Maria Ostrenko

Maria Ostrenko, 55 years old, a retired widow from Omsk. She spent her entire life in Omsk – working as a shop assistant in a household goods store, raising a son who became a contract soldier. W Her son fell in the winter of 2024 during the fighting in Donbas – only an official notice arrived, and his urn was brought home by train.
